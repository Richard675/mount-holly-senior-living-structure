Why Mount Holly was created

A home worth creating today.

25+ yearsin healthcare before founding Mount Holly

After more than 25 years in healthcare, I began thinking about my own future and asking myself:

When I grow older, what do I want my life to look like; and what kind of place would I want to call home?

I imagined a small, intimate community where no one has to feel lost or isolated. A place where we can share meals, build genuine friendships, laugh together, support one another, stay connected to the people we love, and continue enjoying the things that make life meaningful.

I wanted to create today the kind of future I would want for myself; a home where I can age with dignity and grace, be truly known, and receive the high standard of personalized, compassionate care I would expect for myself and want for the people I love.

That vision became Mount Holly Senior Living; a home I would one day be proud to live in myself.
