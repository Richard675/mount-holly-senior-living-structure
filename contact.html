Good to know

Questions families often ask.

Something else on your mind? Add it to your message and we’ll answer personally.

Is my preferred visit date confirmed when I send the form?

Not yet. Your preferred date is a request rather than a booked appointment. Our team will follow up with you to confirm a time that works.

How many people live at Mount Holly?

Mount Holly is a private residential home for six residents, and each resident has their own private, single-occupancy room.

What kind of care is available?

Residents receive 24-hour personal care and support, individual care planning, medication management, and coordination with qualified health professionals, all guided by experienced nursing leadership. See all eleven services.

Can family members come along?

Yes. Family is part of everyday life here, and you are welcome to bring a loved one to your visit.

What should I leave out of the form?

Please don’t include medical records or sensitive health information. We are happy to talk through care needs in person or by phone once we connect.
